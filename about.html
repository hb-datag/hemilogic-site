About

Built where the math and the clinic meet

HemiLogic is a wearable neurotechnology venture based in Cincinnati, founded on a specific technical conviction about how machine intelligence should behave in neurological care.

Mission

Make neurological disease continuously measurable, and make the measurement defensible

Neurological conditions are among the leading causes of disability worldwide, and the tools used to manage them still depend heavily on episodic assessment and patient recall. The gap between what happens to a patient and what a clinician can see is where progression hides.

Closing that gap needs two things at once: sensing a patient will actually wear, and inference a clinician will actually accept. HemiLogic is being built around both constraints rather than solving one and hoping the other follows.

The name. Hemi for the hemispheres of the brain. Logic for the fuzzy inference that reads them. The overlapping curves in the mark are membership functions, the mathematics the platform runs on, with the neural signal traced through them.

Principles

How we intend to build

Interpretability is not optional

If a clinician cannot follow the reasoning, the output does not ship. This constrains our model choices on purpose.

One defensible product first

A narrow indication done rigorously beats a platform that claims four and validates none.

Evidence before claims

Marketing language will not run ahead of what the data and the regulatory pathway actually support.
